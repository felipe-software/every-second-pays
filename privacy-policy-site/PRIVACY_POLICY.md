# Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026

Every Second Pays is developed and operated by Felipe Software. The app is designed to work without accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, or backend servers.

## Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Information entered in the app, such as income and payment schedule details, is stored locally on your device. App preferences, including language and appearance settings, are also stored locally. We cannot access this information.

## Device backups

Your operating system may include the app's local data in device backups. These backups are managed by your device and platform provider and are subject to their privacy policies and your backup settings.

## External links

The app may open links to third-party websites. Those websites operate independently and are governed by their own privacy policies.

## Data deletion

You can delete payment sources from within the app. Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored data from your device, although copies may remain in device backups managed by your platform provider.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact Felipe Software at [hey@felipe.software](mailto:hey@felipe.software).
